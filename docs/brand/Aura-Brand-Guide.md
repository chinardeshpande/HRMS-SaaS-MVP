# Aura Brand Guide

**Version:** 2.0
**Brand:** Aura
**Endorsement:** by ACV Solutions
**Primary descriptor:** People Operations Platform
**Primary tagline:** People operations. Humanly intelligent.
**Supporting promise:** Clarity for every people journey.

## Brand Idea

Aura brings clarity, continuity and human judgment to everyday people operations.

The approved mark is a continuous employee journey shaped into an `A`. Its loop represents connection and context; its forward right stroke represents confident progress. The supplied geometry, proportions, tonal treatment, wordmark and spacing are the source of truth and must not be redrawn or reinterpreted.

## Brand Platform

### Positioning

Aura is the people operations platform that combines dependable HR workflows, connected company knowledge and context-aware assistance in one calm working environment.

### Promise

**Clarity for every people journey.**

### Personality

- Calm, never cold.
- Intelligent, never theatrical.
- Direct, never abrupt.
- Human, never sentimental.
- Confident, never inflated.

### Brand Story

People operations are full of movement: joining, belonging, growing, changing and sometimes leaving. Aura connects those moments without losing the person inside the process. It gives HR teams dependable control, leaders timely clarity and employees a simpler path through work.

## Naming

- Use **Aura** as the product name.
- Pair the approved Aura logo with the separate endorsement **by ACV Solutions** on landing pages, login, proposals, social artwork, launch communication and other first-touch material.
- Use **Aura** alone inside authenticated product navigation after ownership has been established.
- Use **People Operations Platform** as the category descriptor.
- Do not use `Aura HR`, `AuraHR`, `Aurora`, or `AuroraHR` in newly branded customer-facing material.
- Keep technical identifiers, package names, database values and legacy URLs unchanged until the migration plan explicitly covers them.

## Clear Space And Minimum Size

- Full approved logo: minimum 96 px wide.
- Logo paired with the ACV endorsement: minimum 132 px wide.
- Standalone mark: minimum 24 px.
- Favicon artwork: use the provided optical-size asset at 16, 32 or 64 px.
- Clear space around a full logo: at least the height of the `U` cross-section.
- Clear space around the mark: at least one ribbon stroke width.

Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, recolour individual pieces, redraw the crossing, or place the logo over visually busy content.

## Typography

### Plus Jakarta Sans

Use for display headings, section titles and decisive product statements. Prefer weights 600–800.

### Inter

Use for interface text, data tables, forms, supporting copy and long-form reading. Prefer weights 400–700.

Do not tighten letter spacing. Use natural spacing for product text. The approved logo image contains its own deliberate wordmark spacing.

## Language

Aura sounds calm, direct, perceptive and human. The tagline is a brand signal, not a licence to label every feature “intelligent.”

### Prefer

- “Six employee records need your attention.”
- “Lopamudra’s annual CTC is INR 9,00,000.”
- “Review the document before publishing.”
- “I could not verify that from the available records.”

### Avoid

- Inflated AI claims.
- Cheerleading after routine actions.
- Long explanations before the answer.
- Vague labels such as “magic”, “revolutionary” or “all-in-one”.
- Calling employees “resources”.

## Motion

- The ribbon may draw on once during a launch or loading moment.
- Standard interface movement: 160–240 ms.
- Use gentle ease-out transitions.
- Manu may retain subtle state-aware motion.
- Never continuously rotate, bounce or distort the Aura mark.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Product Application

- Use the approved full Aura logo in expanded desktop navigation.
- Use the mark in collapsed navigation and mobile app chrome.
- Use Aura Navy for headings and shell anchors.
- Use Cobalt for primary commands.
- Use Teal for successful or healthy states.
- Use Coral for actionable exceptions, not generic decoration.
- Keep operational screens dense, organised and restrained.

## Landing Application

- The first viewport must identify **Aura** prominently.
- Use “People operations. Humanly intelligent.” as the primary landing tagline.
- Use “Clarity for every people journey.” as supporting copy, not as a substitute for the product name.
- Use real application screens and real employee-operation scenarios.
- Avoid abstract gradient artwork as the main product proof.
- Pair the approved logo with the ACV Solutions endorsement in navigation and footer during the transition period.

## Migration Guardrail

Brand migration should be separated into:

1. Visible product identity and metadata.
2. Customer communication and generated content.
3. Infrastructure identifiers, domains, email addresses and backward compatibility.

Do not perform a blind repository-wide replacement. Legacy names may be part of URLs, database values, environment variables, test fixtures and customer records.
